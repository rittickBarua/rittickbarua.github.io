<p class="statement">Product owner and engineer for <span class="accent">AI systems</span>.</p>

I am an AI product owner, product engineer and software developer. I own AI products end to end: framing the problem, designing the architecture, building the system and taking it through release into everyday use.
{: .lede}

I specialise in language models and NLP (retrieval, agentic workflows and MCP servers), in computer vision and image processing (U-Net segmentation, CNNs and object detection), and in applied machine learning (XGBoost and time-series forecasting).

I am currently Head of Product & Delivery at Rud Pedersen Group, where I led the Chat:R platform from proof of concept to production across 20 offices. Before that I was Head of Applied Science at Grant Thornton, where I authored the UK Government (DSIT) report *Cyber Security Risks to AI*. My PhD is from the University of Cambridge.
