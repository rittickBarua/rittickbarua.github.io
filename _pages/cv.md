## Summary

AI strategy and product leader with a hands-on engineering background spanning enterprise transformation, agentic AI architecture and secure production systems. Head of Product & Delivery for an AI platform serving a 1,000+ person European consultancy: lead C-suite decision-making on AI investment, operating models and 6 to 24-month direction while retaining ownership of product and technical delivery. Former Head of Applied Science; authored the UK Government (DSIT) report *Cyber Security Risks to AI*, with a PhD from the University of Cambridge.
